✦ Monday • Today’s Key Verse ✦

“He gives power to the faint, and to him who has no might he increases strength.”

Isaiah 40:29

When we feel most empty and depleted, God is not finished with us. His strength flows into our weakness like water into a dry vessel.

✦ Reading

Isaiah 40:28–31 — Strength for the Weary

Isaiah reminds us that the Creator of the universe does not grow tired. He sees our exhaustion, He knows our limits — and He offers something the world cannot: renewed strength for those who wait on Him.

“Have you not known? Have you not heard? The Lord is the everlasting God, the Creator of the ends of the earth. He does not faint or grow weary; his understanding is unsearchable. He gives power to the faint, and to him who has no might he increases strength. Even youths shall faint and be weary, and young men shall fall exhausted; but they who wait for the Lord shall renew their strength; they shall mount up with wings like eagles; they shall run and not be weary; they shall walk and not faint.”

Isaiah 40:28–31 (ESV)
