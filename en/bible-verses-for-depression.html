Bible Verses for Heavy Days

Bible Verses for Depression

When the heart feels low, God meets us with nearness, patience, and hope that can be held one breath at a time.

When the Heart Feels Low

Depression can make even simple things feel heavy. Scripture gives words for honest sorrow and gently points the soul toward hope.

Why art thou cast down, O my soul? and why art thou disquieted within me? hope thou in God.

Psalm 42:11

The LORD is nigh unto them that are of a broken heart; and saveth such as be of a contrite spirit.

Psalm 34:18

Come unto me, all ye that labour and are heavy laden, and I will give you rest.

Matthew 11:28

A Short Prayer for Depression

Lord, I feel tired and low. Please stay near to me, steady my thoughts, and help me receive one small light of hope today. Amen.
