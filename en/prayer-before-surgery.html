Prayer Before Surgery

The hours before surgery can hold many emotions. These prayers offer words for peace, wisdom, protection, and recovery.

Prayer for Peace

Lord, calm my heart as I prepare for surgery. Replace fear with Your peace and remind me that You are present in this room and in every moment ahead. Amen.

Prayer for the Medical Team

God, guide the surgeon, nurses, anesthesiologist, and every person who will care for me. Give them wisdom, steady hands, clear judgment, and compassion. Amen.

Prayer for Recovery

Father, protect my body through this procedure. Grant healing, strength, patience, and the support I need during recovery. Help me trust You one day at a time. Amen.
