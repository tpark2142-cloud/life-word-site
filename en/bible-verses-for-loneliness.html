God Is Near

Bible Verses for Loneliness

Loneliness can make the heart feel unseen. Scripture reminds us that God stays near, receives us, and never abandons His people.

When You Feel Alone

Being alone and feeling lonely are not always the same. In either place, God invites us to speak honestly and rest in His faithful presence.

“I will never leave thee, nor forsake thee.”

Hebrews 13:5

“When my father and my mother forsake me, then the LORD will take me up.”

Psalm 27:10

“Fear thou not; for I am with thee: be not dismayed; for I am thy God.”

Isaiah 41:10

A Short Prayer for Loneliness

Lord, I feel alone. Help me notice Your presence and receive the people and small signs of care You place around me. Hold my heart and guide me toward safe, loving community. Amen.
