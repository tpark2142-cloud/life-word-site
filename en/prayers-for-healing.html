Prayer for Every Season

Prayers for Healing

These short prayers can help you begin when words feel difficult. Use them for yourself, a loved one, or someone you are caring for.

Prayer for Physical Healing

Lord, I ask for Your healing hand over this body. Bring strength where there is weakness, peace where there is fear, and hope for each day. Amen.

Prayer Before Treatment

God, give wisdom to the doctors, nurses, and caregivers. Guide every decision and calm every anxious thought. Let this time be held in Your mercy. Amen.

Prayer for a Loved One

Lord, please be near to the one I love. Comfort them, strengthen them, and help our family walk this road with faith, patience, and love. Amen.
