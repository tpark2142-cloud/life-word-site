Prayer for Someone You Love

Prayer for a Sick Family Member

When someone we love is ill, concern can feel heavy. These prayers help families ask for healing, wisdom, endurance, and peace.

Prayer for Healing

Lord, please draw near to my family member. Ease pain, restore strength, guide every treatment, and bring healing according to Your loving wisdom. Amen.

Prayer for the Family

God, give our family patience, unity, and courage. Help us speak gently, make wise decisions, and care for one another without losing hope. Amen.

Prayer for Caregivers

Father, strengthen every caregiver. Give them rest when they are weary, wisdom when choices are difficult, and people who will share the burden. Amen.
