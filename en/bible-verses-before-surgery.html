Bible Verses Before Surgery

Before surgery, Scripture helps the heart remember that God is present in the waiting room, the operating room, and the healing days after.

When Surgery Is Ahead

It is natural to feel afraid before surgery. These verses can help you pray for peace, skilled hands, and steady recovery.

Fear thou not; for I am with thee: be not dismayed; for I am thy God.

Isaiah 41:10

The LORD will strengthen him upon the bed of languishing: thou wilt make all his bed in his sickness.

Psalm 41:3

Commit thy way unto the LORD; trust also in him; and he shall bring it to pass.

Psalm 37:5

A Short Prayer Before Surgery

Lord, I place this surgery in Your hands. Guide the doctors and nurses, calm my heart, and give my body strength to heal. Amen.
