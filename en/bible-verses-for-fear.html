Courage for Today

Bible Verses for Fear

Fear often grows when the way ahead is unclear. God does not ask us to pretend; He calls us to remember His presence and take the next faithful step.

When Fear Takes Hold

Biblical courage is not the absence of fear. It is choosing to trust God while fear is still present.

“Fear thou not; for I am with thee: be not dismayed; for I am thy God.”

Isaiah 41:10

“What time I am afraid, I will trust in thee.”

Psalm 56:3

“For God hath not given us the spirit of fear; but of power, and of love, and of a sound mind.”

2 Timothy 1:7

A Short Prayer When Afraid

God, You know what frightens me. Quiet my thoughts, steady my heart, and remind me that I do not face this moment alone. Give me wisdom and courage for the next step. Amen.
