How to Pray

ðŸªœ The A.C.T.S. Method

A — ADORATION

Begin by praising God for who He is — not for what He’s done, but simply for His nature and character.

C — CONFESSION

Bring your sins honestly before God. He already knows them — confession is for your freedom, not His information.

T — THANKSGIVING

Thank God for specific blessings. Let gratitude redirect your heart from what you lack to what you have been given.

S — SUPPLICATION

Now bring your requests — for yourself and for others. God invites you to ask. He loves to give.
